# Module report — exact set-propagation (T1), delay-bounded detectability (T2), dependence-robust FAR (T3), stealthy set (T4)

All notation is the paper's (`paper/main.tex`): $\stack e=\Gnu(\stack\nu+\stack\delta)$, $Q$ orthonormal basis of $\Range(\Gz)^\perp$, $\widetilde W=\Sigma_Q^{-1/2}$, $C_j$ the $j$-th block column of $Q^\T\Gnu$, deployed threshold $\gamma^{\mathrm{det}}_{k,N}=\sum_j(\bar\mu_j+\bar\delta(h_j))\|\widetilde WC_j\|$, conformal layer as in Theorem 1. Write $\mu_j:=\bar\mu_j+\bar\delta(h_j)>0$, $A:=\widetilde WQ^\T\Gnu\in\R^{d\times Nm_z}$, $d:=Nm_z-r_0$, block columns $A_j:=\widetilde WC_j\in\R^{d\times m_z}$ (blocks with $\mu_j\|A_j\|=0$ deleted w.l.o.g.).

## T1. Exact worst case, certified conservatism

**Definition.** $\gamma^{\mathrm{ex}}_{k,N}:=\max\{\|A\stack m\|:\ \|m_j\|\le\mu_j\ \forall j\}$.

### Theorem T1.1 (variational characterization and attainment)
$$\gamma^{\mathrm{ex}}_{k,N}=\max_{\|u\|=1}\ \varphi(u),\qquad \varphi(u):=\sum_{j=1}^{N}\mu_j\|A_j^\T u\|,$$
optimal perturbation $m_j^\star=\mu_j A_j^\T u^\star/\|A_j^\T u^\star\|$. And $\gamma^{\mathrm{ex}}\le\gamma^{\mathrm{det}}=\sum_j\mu_j\|A_j\|$ with equality **iff** $\bigcap_j\mathbb U_j\neq\{0\}$ ($\mathbb U_j$ = top left singular subspace of $A_j$).
*Proof.* Norm duality $\|A\stack m\|=\max_{\|u\|=1}\sum_j\langle A_j^\T u,m_j\rangle$; exchange the two sups; inner ball-max by Cauchy–Schwarz. Equality analysis: $\|A_j^\T u\|=\|A_j\|$ for unit $u$ iff $u\in\mathbb U_j$. ∎
*Interpretation:* $\gamma^{\mathrm{det}}$ exact iff all whitened dominant directions align; whitening generically destroys alignment.

### Lemma T1.2 (greedy-sign lower bound)
$x_j:=\mu_jA_jw_j$ ($w_j$ top right singular vector). Sequential signs $\epsilon_t=\operatorname{sign}\langle x_t,s_{t-1}\rangle$ give $\|\sum\epsilon_jx_j\|^2\ge\sum\|x_j\|^2$, so
$$\gamma^{\mathrm{ex}}\ \ge\ \gamma^{\mathrm{rss}}:=\big(\textstyle\sum_j\mu_j^2\|A_j\|^2\big)^{1/2}\ \ge\ \gamma^{\mathrm{det}}/\sqrt N.$$

### Theorem T1.3 (computable sandwich; SDP)
$(\gamma^{\mathrm{sdp}})^2:=\max\{\langle A^\T A,M\rangle: M\succeq0,\ \operatorname{tr}M_{jj}\le\mu_j^2\}$. Then $\gamma^{\mathrm{rss}}\le\gamma^{\mathrm{ex}}\le\gamma^{\mathrm{sdp}}\le\gamma^{\mathrm{det}}$.
*Proof.* Lower: T1.2 + rank-one feasible $M$. Upper (weak duality): $\lambda_j=\gamma^{\mathrm{det}}\|A_j\|/\mu_j$ satisfies $A^\T A\preceq\blkdiag(\lambda_jI)$ via triangle + Cauchy–Schwarz split $\|A\stack m\|^2\le(\sum_j\|A_j\|\|m_j\|)^2\le\gamma^{\mathrm{det}}\sum_j(\|A_j\|/\mu_j)\|m_j\|^2$; dual objective $=(\gamma^{\mathrm{det}})^2$. ∎
*Remark:* SDP is the standard relaxation over intersection of N centered ellipsoids; NRT-1999 tightness factor $2\ln(2N\min\{N,m_z\})$ — **quoted from memory, must verify before citing**.

### Proposition T1.4 (NP-hardness)
Exact $\gamma^{\mathrm{ex}}$ NP-hard already for $m_z=1$, $\mu_j\equiv1$: convex max over $[-1,1]^N$ attained at vertices $\{\pm1\}^N$; PSD quadratic over hypercube contains MAX-CUT (Laplacian PSD). ∎

### Algorithm + Corollary T1.5 (convergent ascent; online certified conservatism ratio)
Ascent: $u^0$ from greedy-sign witness; $g^t=\sum_j\mu_jA_jA_j^\T u^t/\|A_j^\T u^t\|\in\partial\varphi$; $u^{t+1}=g^t/\|g^t\|$. (a) monotone by convexity + linear maximization; (b) converges to first-order stationary value (global not guaranteed, T1.4); (c) every iterate certifies $\gamma^{\mathrm{lb}}_t=\varphi(u^t)\le\gamma^{\mathrm{ex}}$, $\gamma^{\mathrm{lb}}_0\ge\gamma^{\mathrm{rss}}$.
**Corollary:** $\hat\rho_k:=\gamma^{\mathrm{det}}/\gamma^{\mathrm{lb}}_{T_{it}}$ satisfies $1\le\gamma^{\mathrm{det}}/\gamma^{\mathrm{ex}}\le\hat\rho_k\le\gamma^{\mathrm{det}}/\gamma^{\mathrm{rss}}\le\sqrt N$ deterministically per window. Cost $O(N^2m_z^2)$/iteration, 3–5 iterations; SDP optional offline diagnostic.

## T2. Detection-delay theorem

Setting: class-$c$ fault, persistent $f_0\ne0$ from $t_f$; windows slide by 1; $d(k)$ = active suffix slots, suffix indicator $\iota_d$; class timing offset $a_c\in\{0,1\}$.
**Branch growth function:** $g_{b,c}(d;k):=\sigma_{\min}(P_b\widetilde WQ^\T\bm F_c(\iota_d\otimes I_{m_c}))$, $\underline g(d):=\inf_{k\in(t_f,t_f+N+a_c]}g_{b,c}(d;k)$ — online computable, need not be monotone in $d$.
Assumptions: (D1) A5′ on horizon windows; (D2) uniform threshold bound $\bar\gamma<\infty$; (D3) calibration event $E_{cal}$ over horizon, $\Pr\{E_{cal}^c\}\le(\lceil N/L\rceil+1)\alpha$ (+T3 slack); (D4) horizon anchors pre-onset & safe ($N_c\ge N_a$); (D5) first-order decomposition valid, bilinear remainder in fault-free content (dispersion gate as hypothesis).

### Theorem T2.1
$D:=\min\{d:\ \underline g(d)\|f_0\|>2\bar\gamma\}$. If $D<\infty$: on $E_{cal}$, branch $b$ alarms at window ending $k^\star=t_f+D+a_c$; delay $\le D+a_c$. $d=N$ recovers Corollary 1 (eq. mdf).
*Proof.* Window at $k^\star$ has $D$ active suffix slots, anchor pre-onset; split $P_b\widetilde WQ^\T\stack e=\bm w^h+\bm v_D$, $\|\bm v_D\|\ge\underline g(D)\|f_0\|$, $\|\bm w^h\|\le\bar\gamma$; reverse triangle: $T_b\ge 2\bar\gamma-\bar\gamma=\bar\gamma\ge\gamma^{\mathrm{dyn}}$. ∎

### Corollary T2.2 (solvable sufficient version)
Whitened persistent excitation $\underline g(d)\ge c_\sigma\sqrt d$ for $d\ge d_0$ ⟹ $D\le\max\{d_0,\lceil 4\bar\gamma^2/(c_\sigma^2\|f_0\|^2)\rceil\}$ (if $\le N$): delay inverse-quadratic in amplitude. Sufficient structural condition: slot maps $\|T_jf_0\|\ge c_1\|f_0\|$ and pairwise $\langle T_if_0,T_jf_0\rangle\ge0$ ⟹ $c_\sigma=c_1$.
**Honesty:** (i) closed-loop compensation = violation of non-cancellation; $c_\sigma$ computed per regime from frozen operators; $D=\infty$ possible/reportable = compensated-fault blindness; (ii) transient part: subtract $\|P_b\widetilde WQ^\T\bm F_c\|\|\tilde{\bm f}\|$; (iii) m-of-n latching adds $\le n-1$.

## T3. FAR under β-mixing

Assumptions: (M1) fault-free excess-score process strictly stationary (frozen score map, replayed alarm-independent anchor policy, block boundaries aligned with anchor-age cycle); (M2) β-mixing with β **defined** as TV(joint, product) — no ½-convention exposure; (M3) $n$ calibration blocks length $L$, gaps $\ge g$ (real time), online block same; Mondrian per cell.

### Lemma T3.1 (TV blocking)
Blocks with gaps $\ge g$: $\mathrm{TV}(\mathcal L(B_1..B_m),\otimes\mathcal L(B_i))\le(m-1)\beta(g)$. Induction; tensoring with same factor does not increase TV. ∎

### Theorem T3.2
$\Pr\{\tau^\ast>\hat q\}\le\alpha+n\beta(g)$ (and pointwise alarm same bound).
*Proof.* Alarm event measurable in $n+1$ blocks; TV blocking → product measure; under product, block maxima iid (M1) → exchangeable → split-conformal rank ≤ α. ∎
**Practical rule (Sec III-E):** budget $n\beta(g)\le\alpha/10$; $g$ from conservative mixing proxy $\hat\beta$ (labeled diagnostic — β not identifiable); $L\ge g$; $n=\lfloor T_{cal}/(L+g)\rfloor\ge\lceil1/\alpha\rceil-1$. Theorem's role: graceful degradation of Theorem 1.

## T4. Stealthy set outer bound

Constraints: post-onset $\|u_k\|\le u_{\max}$, $\|\Delta u_k\|\le\Delta u_{\max}$; pre-onset $\|u\|\le\bar u_0$; $\|\Delta\xi\|\le\bar\xi$; $\bar\delta(h)\le\bar\delta_{\max}$.
Definitions: $S_C=\sum_j\|\widetilde WC_j\|$; $\bar\mu^{\mathrm{sat}}(h)=\max_c[\eta_0+\eta_u\min\{u_{\max},\bar u_0+h\Delta u_{\max}\}+\eta_{\Delta u}\Delta u_{\max}+\eta_\xi\bar\xi]+\bar\delta_{\max}$; $\bar q_b=\sigma_bq^0_b+Q^{\mathrm{gap}}_b$ (max inter-cell step, precomputed); $\bm v_k$ = observable whitened annihilated signature (reference-relative).

### Proposition T4.1 (stealthy set)
$\varsigma(h):=2[\bar\mu^{\mathrm{sat}}(h)S_C+\bar q_0]$; $\mathbb S_{\mathrm{stealth}}:=\{\{\bm v_k\}:\|\bm v_k\|\le\varsigma(k-t_f)\ \forall k\}$. Under A5′ + calibration event + first-order gate:
1. No alarm at $k$ ⟹ $\|\bm v_k\|\le\varsigma(k-t_f)$; indefinitely undetected ⟹ trajectory ∈ $\mathbb S_{\mathrm{stealth}}$; conversely exceeding $\varsigma$ at one window forces alarm.
2. $\varsigma(h)\uparrow\varsigma_\infty=2[\bar\mu^{\mathrm{sat}}(\infty)S_C+\bar q_0]$; below saturation the ceiling grows linearly with slope $2\eta_u\Delta u_{\max}S_C$ — masking takes rate-limited time.
3. Over $M$ disjoint stealthy windows: $\sum\|\bm v\|^2\le M\varsigma_\infty^2$.
*Proof.* Triangle both ways on the split $\bm w^h+\bm v_k$; envelope bounds per slot; quantile ≤ $\bar q_0$ (cells don't condition on $\|u\|$). ∎
**Honesty:** outer bound only (membership ≠ certified stealth); conditional on per-window calibration events (not a.s.); $\bm v_k$ reference-relative — sub-warning anchor contamination outside scope (declared blind class).
