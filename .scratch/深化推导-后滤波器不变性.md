# Post-Filter Module: Invariance-Theoretic Optimality — Complete Derivations

Notation is that of `paper/main.tex`: stacked residual $\bm e=\bm e_{s,N}\in\R^{Nm_z}$, anchor stack $\Gz\in\R^{Nm_z\times m_z}$ with block rows $\Phi(s{+}l,s)$, $r_0=\rank\Gz=\rank A_s$, annihilator basis $Q\in\R^{Nm_z\times(Nm_z-r_0)}$ orthonormal with $\Range(Q)=\Range(\Gz)^{\perp}$, $\Sigma_Q=Q^{\T}\Sigma_{\mathrm e}Q$, $\widetilde W=\Sigma_Q^{-1/2}$ (symmetric PD root), actuator stack $\Fa=\Gnu\,\mathrm{blkdiag}(B^u_s,\dots,B^u_{s+N-1})$, branch statistics $T_0,T_{\mathrm g},T_c$ as in Eq. (bank).

## 0. Standing assumptions

**Assumption (I) — idealized window model (certainty-equivalence reduction).** On a fixed window (anchor $s$, length $N$), the stacked residual obeys
$$\bm e=\Gz\,\eta+\bm n+\bm F\bm f,$$
where (i) $\eta\in\R^{m_z}$ is an unknown deterministic anchor-error nuisance; (ii) $\bm n\sim\mathcal N(0,\Sigma_{\mathrm e})$, $\Sigma_{\mathrm e}\succ0$ known (frozen estimate), with true cross-time correlation; (iii) $\bm F\bm f$ is the first-order class signature, $\bm f=0$ under $H_0$; (iv) the operators $\Phi,\Gz,\Gnu,\Fa$ are frozen (evaluated along the protected trajectory, treated as noise-independent); (v) deterministic mismatch/context terms and the bilinear remainder are set to zero — in deployment carried by $\gamma^{\mathrm{det}}$ and the conformal layer, which own the level regardless of (I).

**Assumption (G) — bounded-nuisance variant (for T3).** Same as (I) but $\|\eta\|\le\bar e$ with $\bar e$ the calibrated anchor-error envelope, and $\bm n$ need only be zero-mean with known (calibratable) quantile function $q_n(\cdot)$ of $\|\bm n\|$; no Gaussianity required.

The unbounded nuisance of (I) is the idealization under which annihilation is forced (T1–T2); the bounded model (G) is the honest physical model under which annihilation over-kills and the guard branch earns its keep (T3).

## T1. The annihilator is the canonical invariance reduction

**Theorem PF-A (maximal invariant of the anchor-translation group).**
Let $\mathbb V_0=\Range(\Gz)$, $\dim\mathbb V_0=r_0=\rank A_s\le m_z$, and let
$$\mathcal G_0=\{g_w:\ \bm r\mapsto\bm r+w,\ \ w\in\mathbb V_0\}$$
be the translation group generated by the anchor nuisance ($w=\Gz\eta$; the parameterization is non-injective when $r_0<m_z$, but the group is exactly the set of translations by $\mathbb V_0$). Let $Q$ be any orthonormal basis of $\mathbb V_0^{\perp}$. Then:

(a) $M(\bm r)=Q^{\T}\bm r$ is invariant.
(b) $M$ is a **maximal** invariant: $M(\bm r_1)=M(\bm r_2)\Rightarrow \bm r_1=g_w\bm r_2$ for some $w\in\mathbb V_0$.
(c) Every $\mathcal G_0$-invariant statistic is a function of $M$: $\varphi=h\circ M$ with $h(m)=\varphi(Qm)$.
(d) Canonicality: any two orthonormal bases $Q,Q'$ satisfy $Q'=QU$ with $U$ orthogonal; whitened statistics are basis-independent: $\|\widetilde W'Q'^{\T}\bm r\|=\|\widetilde WQ^{\T}\bm r\|$.
(e) Under (I) with unrestricted $\eta$, the composite testing problem is invariant under $\mathcal G_0$; hence the class of invariant tests is exactly the class of tests based on $Q^{\T}\bm e$ — the annihilator is the invariance reduction, not a design heuristic.

*Proof.* (a) $Q^{\T}(\bm r+w)=Q^{\T}\bm r$ since $Q^{\T}w=0$ for $w\in\mathbb V_0$. (b) $Q^{\T}(\bm r_1-\bm r_2)=0$ gives $\bm r_1-\bm r_2\in\ker Q^{\T}=\mathbb V_0$. (c) With $QQ^{\T}\bm r=\bm r-\Pi_{\mathbb V_0}\bm r$ and invariance, $h(M(\bm r))=\varphi(\bm r-\Pi_{\mathbb V_0}\bm r)=\varphi(\bm r)$. (d) $\Sigma_{Q'}=U^{\T}\Sigma_QU$, $\Sigma_{Q'}^{-1/2}=U^{\T}\Sigma_Q^{-1/2}U$, norms preserved. (e) $g_w$ maps $\mathcal N(\Gz\eta+\bm F\bm f,\Sigma_{\mathrm e})$ to $\mathcal N(\Gz(\eta+\eta')+\bm F\bm f,\Sigma_{\mathrm e})$, same hypothesis class. $\blacksquare$

**Remark PF-A1 (rank-deficient cases).** For $r_0<m_z$ (singular $A_s$) the group is the translation group of the $r_0$-dimensional $\mathbb V_0$; the annihilator removes exactly what the nuisance can reach. For $r_0=0$, $Q=I$, reduction vacuous.

**Remark PF-A2 (energy-thresholded partial annihilation).** Implementation annihilates only $\mathbb V_1\subseteq\mathbb V_0$ (directions whose gain exceeds the noise floor). $Q_1^{\T}\bm e$ is the maximal invariant of the subgroup of translations along $\mathbb V_1$; retained anchor directions are low-gain, hence a *bounded* nuisance per (G), priced by the threshold generator as the guard prices $\mathbb V_0$. All statements hold with $\mathbb V_0\to\mathbb V_1$.

## T2. GLR/UMPI structure of the branch bank

After reduction and whitening: $m=\widetilde WQ^{\T}\bm e\sim\mathcal N(\mu,I_d)$, $d=Nm_z-r_0$, $\mu=\widetilde WQ^{\T}\bm F\bm f$. Matched class $c$: $\mu\in\Sspace{c}\setminus\{0\}$, $\dim\Sspace c=d_c<d$. Omnibus: $\mu\neq0$ arbitrary.

**Proposition PF-B1 (GLR identities).** $2\log\mathrm{GLR}_c=\|\Pi_{\Sspace{c}}m\|^2=T_c^2$; $2\log\mathrm{GLR}_{\mathrm{omni}}=\|m\|^2=T_0^2$.
*Proof.* $2[\log p_\mu-\log p_0]=\|m\|^2-\|m-\mu\|^2$; maximize over $\mu\in\Sspace c$ at $\mu^\ast=\Pi_{\Sspace c}m$, Pythagoras. $\blacksquare$

**Lemma PF-B2 (MLR of noncentral $\chi^2$).** $\{\chi^2_k(\delta)\}_{\delta\ge0}$ has monotone likelihood ratio in $t$.
*Proof.* Poisson-mixture $f_\delta=f_k\cdot g_\delta$, $g_\delta(t)=\sum_j a_j(\delta)t^j$ with coefficient ratios $a_j(\delta_2)/a_j(\delta_1)=e^{-(\delta_2-\delta_1)/2}(\delta_2/\delta_1)^j$ increasing in $j$; the cross-term expansion $g_{\delta_2}(t)g_{\delta_1}(s)-g_{\delta_2}(s)g_{\delta_1}(t)=\sum_{j>i}[a_j(\delta_2)a_i(\delta_1)-a_i(\delta_2)a_j(\delta_1)](t^js^i-t^is^j)\ge0$ for $t>s>0$. $\blacksquare$

**Theorem PF-B3 (UMPI matched branch).** Let $\mathcal G_c$ be generated by (i) anchor translations $\mathcal G_0$ and (ii) rotations of the whitened annihilated space fixing $\Sspace{c}$ setwise, acting on $\bm e$ via $Q^{\T}\bm e\mapsto\widetilde W^{-1}U\widetilde W\,Q^{\T}\bm e$ (which preserves $\Sigma_Q$ since $\widetilde W\Sigma_Q\widetilde W=I$). For $H_0:\mu=0$ vs $H_c:\mu\in\Sspace{c}\setminus\{0\}$, the test $T_c^2>c_\alpha$ with $\Pr\{\chi^2_{d_c}>c_\alpha\}=\alpha$ is UMPI simultaneously for every $\lambda=\|\mu\|>0$, a.e. unique, with power strictly increasing in $\lambda$.
*Proof.* Step 1: translation reduction (PF-A). Step 2: $(t_1,t_2)=(\|\Pi_{\Sspace c}m\|^2,\|\Pi_{\Sspace c^\perp}m\|^2)$ is maximal invariant of the rotation group (transitivity of $O$ on subspace spheres). Step 3: $t_1\sim\chi^2_{d_c}(\lambda^2)\perp t_2\sim\chi^2_{d-d_c}$. Step 4: invariant likelihood ratio $=f_{\lambda^2}(t_1)/f_0(t_1)$ increasing in $t_1$ (PF-B2), independent of $t_2$; NP test $t_1>c_\alpha$ same for all $\lambda$. $\blacksquare$

**Theorem PF-B4 (omnibus: UMPI + maximin over shells).** For $H_1:\mu\ne0$ under the full rotation group: maximal invariant $\|m\|^2\sim\chi^2_d(\lambda^2)$, test $T_0^2>c_\alpha$ UMPI. Moreover maximin on every shell: $\inf_{\|\mu\|=\lambda}\Pr_\mu\{T_0^2>c_\alpha\}\ge\inf_{\|\mu\|=\lambda}\Pr_\mu\{\text{reject by }\varphi\}$ for every level-$\alpha$ $\varphi$.
*Proof (Hunt–Stein via compact-group averaging).* $\bar\varphi(m)=\int_{O(d)}\varphi(Um)\,\mathrm dH(U)$ is invariant, level $\le\alpha$; its power at $\mu$ averages $\varphi$ over the orbit, bounded below by the orbit infimum; UMPI of $T_0$ dominates $\bar\varphi$. $\blacksquare$

**Proposition PF-B5 (CFAR scope).** Fault-free, under (I): the joint law of all annihilated statistics is independent of $\eta$ — exactly, at every reference age ($Q^{\T}\bm e=Q^{\T}\bm n$). $T_0^2\sim\chi^2_d$, $T_c^2\sim\chi^2_{d_c}$ with $\eta$-free thresholds: CFAR **w.r.t. the anchor nuisance**. NOT claimed: (i) CFAR w.r.t. unknown noise scale — the Scharf–Friedlander $F$-form restores it for matched branches but is unavailable for the omnibus; here delegated to the conformal layer; (ii) CFAR w.r.t. $\Sigma_e$ misspecification — likewise conformal.

**Proposition PF-B6 (max-of-branches: what transfers).**
(i) *No UMP beyond one dimension*: for $d\ge2$, restricting to two non-collinear rays forces two a.e.-different UMP tests — contradiction.
(ii) *Union–intersection identity on the likelihood scale*: $\sup_{\mu\in\cup_c\Sspace c}\log\frac{p_\mu}{p_0}=\tfrac12\max_cT_c^2$. The deployed $\tau_k=\max_b\max\{0,T_b-\gamma^{\mathrm{det}}_b\}/\sigma_b$ is a *weighted* UI statistic, not a GLR: normalizations break the common likelihood scale — deliberate trade for physical normalization + per-branch conformal pricing.
(iii) *No invariance reduction for the union problem* (intersection group too small in general position).
(iv) *Power bracket (positive)*: with calibrated max threshold $q$, $\max_b\Pr_\mu\{s_b>q\}\le\Pr_\mu(A)\le\sum_b\Pr_\mu\{s_b>q\}$; each branch operates at effective level $\alpha_b\le\alpha$; the bank's whole cost is the deflation $\alpha\to\alpha_b$, priced observably by conformal max calibration. Level is distribution-free (Theorem far); optimality statements exact only under (I).

## T3. Guard branch: strict domination on the invariance-blind class

**Proposition PF-C1 (blindness of all invariant tests).** Under (G) (any noise law), if $\bm v=\bm F\bm f\in\mathbb V_0$ then every test that is a function of $Q^{\T}\bm e$ has power identically equal to size, at every fault amplitude ($Q^{\T}\bm e=Q^{\T}\bm n$ regardless of $\eta,\bm v$). Blindness is a property of the statistic class, not the group, so it survives the bounded model.

**Lemma PF-C2 (blind class is physical, nonempty).** If $\Range(B^u_s)\subseteq\Range(A_s)$ (automatic for invertible $A_s$), every pure-first-post-anchor-step actuator impulse has signature in $\mathbb V_0$: $\Fa^{(1)}f_1=\Gz w$ with $A_sw=B^u_sf_1$. Conversely if $\Range(B^u_s)\not\subseteq\Range(A_s)$, some first-step impulses have positive retained fraction.

**Proposition PF-C3 (guard: level + power).** Under (G), guard $T_{\mathrm g}=\|\bm e\|$ with $\gamma_{\mathrm g}=\sigma_{\max}(\Gz)\bar e+q_n(1-\alpha_{\mathrm g})$:
(a) Level uniformly over the nuisance: fault-free $\Pr\{T_{\mathrm g}>\gamma_{\mathrm g}\}\le\alpha_{\mathrm g}$ for every $\|\eta\|\le\bar e$.
(b) Power: for any $\bm v$ with $\|\bm v\|\ge 2\sigma_{\max}(\Gz)\bar e+q_n(1-\alpha_{\mathrm g})+q_n(1-\beta)$, alarm w.p. $\ge1-\beta$, every $\|\eta\|\le\bar e$. ($c_1=2\sigma_{\max}(\Gz)$, $c_2$-term $=$ two noise quantiles.)
*Proof.* Triangle inequalities both ways. $\blacksquare$

**Theorem PF-C4 (strict domination).** Two-branch test $\psi$ (invariant level-$\alpha_0$ + guard level-$\alpha_{\mathrm g}$, $\alpha_0+\alpha_{\mathrm g}\le\alpha$; deployed conformal max achieves level $\le\alpha$ directly). With $\tau^\ast=2\sigma_{\max}(\Gz)\bar e+q_n(1-\alpha_{\mathrm g})+q_n(1-\beta)$ and $\mathfrak B(\tau^\ast)=\{\bm v\in\mathbb V_0:\|\bm v\|\ge\tau^\ast\}$:
(a) size$(\psi)\le\alpha$; (b) power$(\psi)\ge1-\beta$ on $\mathfrak B(\tau^\ast)$; (c) every purely invariant level-$\alpha$ test has power $\le\alpha$ there — $\psi$ strictly dominates with gap $\ge1-\beta-\alpha$, tending to $1-\alpha$; (d) honesty: on $\bm v\notin\mathbb V_0$ the comparison can reverse by at most the level split — class-restricted domination, price = observable quantile inflation. By PF-C2 the class is nonempty and physical.

## T4. Retained-fraction theory for onset-p actuator faults

Definitions: impulse partial stack $\Fa^{(p)}$ (blocks $\Phi(s{+}l,s{+}p)B^u_{s+p-1}$, $l\ge p$); persistent $\Fa^{(p:N)}=\Fa(\bm 1_{[p:N]}\otimes I)$; $\rho(p;f_0)=\|Q^{\T}\bm g_p\|^2/\|\bm g_p\|^2$.

**Theorem PF-D1 (exact computable formula).** With thin SVD $\Gz=U_0\Sigma_0V_0^{\T}$: $\rho=1-\|U_0^{\T}\bm g\|^2/\|\bm g\|^2=\sin^2\theta(\bm g,\mathbb V_0)$, and $U_0^{\T}\bm g=\Sigma_0^{-1}V_0^{\T}(\Gz^{(p:N)})^{\T}\bm g^{(p:N)}$ — computable from partial stacks alone. Class extremes: $\rho\in[\sin^2\theta_1,\sin^2\theta_{\max}]$, $\rho_{\min}(p)=1-\sigma_{\max}^2(U_0^{\T}V_p)$ (principal angles = singular values of $U_0^{\T}V_p$). Whitened sandwich: $\|\widetilde WQ^{\T}\bm g\|^2\in[\rho\|\bm g\|^2/\lambda_{\max}(\Sigma_Q),\rho\|\bm g\|^2/\lambda_{\min}(\Sigma_Q)]$.

**Lemma PF-D2 (pure first step, exact scope).** $\rho_\delta(1;f)=0$ for all $f$ **iff** $\Range(B^u_s)\subseteq\Range(A_s)$. Caution: the **persistent** onset-1 fraction $\rho(1)$ is NOT zero in general; only the step-1 contribution is annihilated.

**Counterexample PF-D3 (monotonicity FALSE, both directions).** $m_z=m_u=1$, $N=3$, $(A_s,A_{s+1},A_{s+2})=(1,-1,1)$, $B^u\equiv1$: $\Gz=(1,-1,-1)^{\T}$, $\bm g_1=(1,0,1)^{\T}$, $\bm g_2=(0,1,2)^{\T}$, $\bm g_3=(0,0,1)^{\T}$, giving $\rho(1)=1$, $\rho(2)=2/5$, $\rho(3)=2/3$ — neither monotone direction holds. The paper's sentence "retained energy fraction of early-step signatures decreases with the accumulated transition gain" is untenable as monotonicity and must be replaced by PF-D4.

**Proposition PF-D4 (monotone certified lower bound; limit).** $1-\rho(p)\le\min\{1,\ (N-p+1)\max_{p\le l\le N}\|\Phi(s{+}l,s)\|^2/\sigma_{r_0}(\Gz)^2\}$ uniformly in direction. Under uniform exponential stability $\|\Phi(s{+}l,s)\|\le C\lambda^l$: $\rho(p)\ge\underline\rho(p)=1-C^2(N-p+1)\lambda^{2p}/\sigma_{r_0}(\Gz)^2$, strictly increasing in $p$, $1-\rho(p)\to0$ geometrically. When $r_0=m_z$: $\sigma_{r_0}(\Gz)\ge\sigma_{\min}(A_s)$.

**Proposition PF-D5 (scalar LTI closed form).** $m_z=m_u=1$, $A\equiv a\ne0$: $\rho_\delta(p)=1-\sum_{m=p-1}^{N-1}a^{2m}/\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}a^{2m}$; $\rho_\delta(1)=0$, strictly increasing in $p$ for every $a\ne0$.

**Corollary PF-D6 (onset-dependent MDF inflation).** Minimum detectable energy of an onset-$p$ actuator fault inflated by at most $\rho(p)^{-1/2}$ relative to hypothetical unannihilated matched detection; factor $\to1$ geometrically (PF-D4).
