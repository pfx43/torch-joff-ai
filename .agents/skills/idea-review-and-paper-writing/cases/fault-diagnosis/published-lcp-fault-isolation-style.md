# Published style exemplar: LCP fault isolation

- Corpus ID: P03
- Paper: *Layer-Wise Contribution-Filtered Propagation for Deep Learning-Based Fault Isolation*
- Case role: published-style exemplar
- Task group: fault isolation and attribution
- Positive-use tier: primary architecture and problem-to-contribution source

## Architecture worth retaining

The Introduction defines the fault-isolation need, synthesizes attribution
methods by mechanism, identifies nonlinearity and stacked-sample conflicts, and
turns them into two focused issues. Section 2 combines preliminaries and problem
formulation; Section 3 gives the core method, task use, and stacked-input
extension; Section 4 validates; Section 5 concludes.

## Curated micro-examples

Source, PDF p. 2:

> “This study is motivated by two fundamental issues...”

Use: convert a literature limitation into an ordered question list immediately
before matched contribution themes.

Source, PDF p. 2:

> “The rest of the article is organized as follows.”

Use: a conventional roadmap only after the contribution logic is complete; do
not use roadmaps as substitutes for transitions inside the method.

## Narrative pattern

`attribution objective -> existing propagation mechanisms -> nonlinear and
multi-attribution defects -> two issues -> sufficient condition/operation ->
fault-isolation consequence -> two-process evidence`

## Do not imitate

Repair subject–verb agreement and avoid `a.k.a.` in formal method introduction
unless the alternative name is genuinely established. A source contribution
statement is not evidence that the same contribution transfers to another model.
