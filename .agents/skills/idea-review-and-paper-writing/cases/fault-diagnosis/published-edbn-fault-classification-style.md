# Published style exemplar: EDBN fault classification

- Corpus ID: P01
- Paper: *A Novel Deep Learning Based Fault Diagnosis Approach for Chemical Process With Extended Deep Belief Network*
- Case role: published-style exemplar
- Task group: fault diagnosis and classification
- Positive-use tier: structural history; modernize local English before reuse

## Architecture worth retaining

The Introduction moves from automated-process safety to FDD, narrows from broad
diagnostic methods to deep learning, identifies layer-wise information loss,
and then introduces EDBN. The body uses background -> model -> training -> two
case-study subsections -> conclusion.

## Curated micro-example

Source, PDF p. 1:

> “To alleviate this problem, an extended deep belief network (EDBN) is proposed...”

Rhetorical function: a concrete limitation immediately precedes the responsive
construction. Reuse the dependency, not the novelty adjective or whole sentence:

`Because [specific loss] occurs under [condition], [construction] is introduced
to preserve or change [quantity].`

## Narrative use

Use this case when a fault-classification paper must narrow a broad industrial
need into one representation-learning defect. Do not begin with EDBN internals;
state the lost information and its diagnostic consequence first.

## Do not imitate

Do not inherit older local grammar, broad superiority wording, or the assumption
that stacking a known architecture is itself a contribution. Verify current
terminology and closest work independently.
